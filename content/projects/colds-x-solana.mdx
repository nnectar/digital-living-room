Secured a Solana Foundation grant and built the first structured developer acquisition pipeline for the Solana ecosystem in West Africa. Designed the full-funnel strategy from community awareness to technical onboarding to project incubation, combining ecosystem partnerships, developer marketing, technical content, educational campaigns, and hands-on technical training to drive ecosystem growth in an emerging market.

## Program

- Partnered with Holaplex (largest decentralized NFT auction provider on Solana) to onboard African creators
- Ran workshops, art contests, and community programming
- Produced **Catalyst 1.0** — a Web3 onboarding event in Lagos sponsored by Jet Protocol
- Launched the **COLDS Cohort** incubation program providing end-to-end Web3 project support: education, creative direction, funding, technical support, and marketing
- Built the **COLDS x Solana Challenger** — a gamified, token-gated web app designed to drive engagement throughout the campaign, rewarding participants for completing onboarding milestones and community challenges
