Led developer education and go-to-market for NEAR Intents as part of NEAR's DevHub team. Designed hackathon challenge tracks, technical resources, and educational content around NEAR's intents-based transaction architecture — where users express what they want to accomplish and the protocol resolves the optimal execution path across chains.

## What NEAR Intents Does

NEAR Intents enables a declarative transaction model:

- **Intent Expression** — users declare desired outcomes rather than specifying exact transaction steps
- **Solver Network** — competitive solvers find optimal execution paths across chains and protocols
- **Cross-Chain Resolution** — intents resolve across multiple blockchains without users managing bridging or routing

## Key Programs

- **Zcash x NEAR Intents Hackathon** — flagship launch vehicle for NEAR's intents architecture, designing challenge tracks and technical resources that helped developers build on cross-chain intent resolution infrastructure
- Developer education content translating intents concepts into practical builder challenges
- Workshop programming at global events introducing the intents paradigm
