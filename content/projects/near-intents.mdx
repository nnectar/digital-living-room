Led developer education and go-to-market for NEAR Intents as part of NEAR's DevHub team. Designed hackathon challenge tracks, technical resources, and educational content around NEAR's intents-based transaction architecture — where users express what they want to accomplish and the protocol resolves the optimal execution path across chains.

## What NEAR Intents Does

NEAR Intents is a cross-chain intent resolution layer where users declare desired outcomes and competitive solvers find the optimal execution path:

- **$29.5B+** in cumulative DEX volume
- **25M+ swaps** processed
- **25–70 chains** and assets connected
- **$2B–$3.6B** in monthly trading volume
- **$10M+** in cumulative fees generated
- Daily volume consistently above **$150M**, a 10x increase quarter-over-quarter

Solvers compete to fill user orders across any chain and any asset, settling in seconds. The architecture abstracts away bridging, routing, and gas management — users just say what they want and the network handles execution.

- Workshop programming at global events introducing the intents paradigm
