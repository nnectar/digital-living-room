Developed and executed the GTM strategy for Forse, an institutional governance intelligence platform — from product positioning and technical documentation to governance proposal pipeline and enterprise adoption. Created a greenfield enterprise pipeline that generated 35 qualified opportunities and resulted in 10 protocol partnerships and integrations within three months. Managed CRM workflows, lead qualification, outbound campaigns, executive follow-up, and partnership reporting across protocol customers.

## What Forse Does

Forse equips DAO operators, delegates, core teams, and service providers with comprehensive performance metrics and insights to drive data-driven decision-making. Two core products — Forse Terminals and Forse Impact Reports — deliver:

- **Impact measurement** — accurately measure the impact of growth initiatives on KPIs
- **User intelligence** — retention, acquisition, and engagement metrics enabling targeted initiatives
- **Value comparison** — per-dollar effectiveness across growth strategies like airdrops, liquidity rewards, and grants
- **Market intelligence** — evidence-based recommendations on where protocols can expand or deploy next

## Context

Built from StableLab's experience leading strategic partnerships across Ethereum's largest protocols — including Uniswap, Lido, Safe, Compound, Arbitrum, and Starknet — collaborating with foundations, treasury managers, delegates, infrastructure providers, and institutional stakeholders responsible for billions in onchain assets. Forse addresses a critical gap: despite DAOs managing billions in capital allocation, they lacked adequate tools for measurement and informed decision-making.
