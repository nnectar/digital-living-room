Led the go-to-market strategy for Forse, the first DAO data and intelligence platform, built by StableLab to address the critical gap between governance decisions and measurable outcomes.

## What Forse Does

Forse equips DAO operators, delegates, core teams, and service providers with comprehensive performance metrics and insights to drive data-driven decision-making. Two core products — Forse Terminals and Forse Impact Reports — deliver:

- **Impact measurement** — accurately measure the impact of growth initiatives on KPIs to evaluate strategy effectiveness and fund deployment
- **User intelligence** — retention, acquisition, and engagement metrics enabling targeted initiatives
- **Value comparison** — per-dollar effectiveness across growth strategies like airdrops, liquidity rewards, and grants
- **Market intelligence** — evidence-based recommendations on where protocols can expand or deploy next
- **Multi-source data integration** — onchain and offchain data sources with private analysis capabilities

## Context

Built from StableLab's experience contributing to governance systems and frameworks across 25+ leading protocols since 2021, Forse addresses a critical gap: despite DAOs managing billions in capital allocation, they lacked adequate tools for measurement and informed decision-making.
