Led the GTM and marketing campaign for PL_Genesis Season 1: Modular Worlds, Protocol Labs' flagship global hackathon series designed to surface the most promising projects across decentralized infrastructure, AI, crypto systems, and emerging internet primitives.

## Results

- **1,900+** builders registered
- **350+** projects shipped
- **$250K+** in prizes awarded
- **20+** teams advanced into Founders Forge accelerator
- **100+** content pieces produced across channels
- **20+** ecosystem partners onboarded

## Program Structure

Three competition tracks — Secure, Sovereign Systems; AI & Autonomous Infrastructure; and Decentralized Economies, Governance & Science — with Fresh Code and Existing Code prize tiers.

Global roadshow spanning Toronto, Ho Chi Minh City, Nairobi, and Berlin, paired with protocol workshops and hacker houses to drive regional builder engagement.
