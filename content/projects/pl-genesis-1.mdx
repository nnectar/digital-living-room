Owned the end-to-end GTM and demand generation strategy for PL_Genesis Season 1: Modular Worlds, Protocol Labs' flagship global hackathon series. Directed marketing campaigns across paid media, partner marketing, email, social, landing pages, and analytics — building the full acquisition pipeline from awareness through registration to project submission and driving conversion across organic, partner, and paid channels.

## Results

- **1,900+** builders registered through multi-channel acquisition funnel
- **350+** projects shipped — 18%+ registration-to-submission conversion
- **$250K+** in prizes awarded
- **20+** teams advanced into Founders Forge accelerator pipeline
- **100+** content pieces produced across channels
- **20+** ecosystem partners onboarded through strategic sponsorship outreach

## Strategy

Designed the demand generation engine across three layers: a multi-channel content strategy spanning technical blogs, social campaigns, ecosystem newsletters, and developer forums with 100+ campaign assets produced; a partner-driven distribution network leveraging 20+ sponsor channels through strategic co-marketing; and a global field marketing roadshow spanning Toronto, Ho Chi Minh City, Nairobi, and Berlin with protocol workshops and hacker houses to drive regional builder pipeline.

Three competition tracks — Secure, Sovereign Systems; AI & Autonomous Infrastructure; and Decentralized Economies, Governance & Science — with Fresh Code and Existing Code prize tiers.
