## Key Programs

- **One Trillion Agents Hackathon** — flagship AI-focused hackathon on NEAR's agent framework, driving builder adoption of autonomous agent infrastructure
- **AI CHAIN NEAR Hackathon (Tunisia)** — international hackathon bringing AI + blockchain development to North African builder communities
- **Supermoon Camp** — builder residency integrating NEAR AI tooling into project prototyping
