## What NEAR AI Does

NEAR AI is confidential, verifiable AI infrastructure for the agentic economy — founded by Illia Polosukhin, co-author of the "Attention Is All You Need" transformer paper. The platform gives developers and enterprises full control over their data through hardware-backed private inference:

- **57+ models** available across the platform, including DeepSeek, GLM, Kimi, and Qwen variants
- **8 confidential models** running in Trusted Execution Environments (TEEs) with 100% hardware-attested requests
- **Enterprise clients** including NVIDIA, Intel, Brave, Venice, and the Government of Bermuda
- **OpenAI-compatible API** — 30-second developer setup
