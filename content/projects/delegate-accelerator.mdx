Designed and executed a governance talent pipeline for Scroll DAO — from proposal authorship and forum-based demand generation through applicant qualification to a structured 10-week training curriculum. Built the full acquisition funnel that sourced, screened, and developed competent governance participants at scale, applying the same strategic partnership and content marketing approach used across StableLab's institutional protocol engagements.

## Results

- **80** applicants for the program
- **30** delegates selected for the cohort
- **10-week** curriculum covering treasury management, proposal evaluation, and onchain governance
- Graduates appeared in [Scroll's Verified Delegates Cohort 1](https://forum.scroll.io/t/verified-delegates-cohort-1/1430)
