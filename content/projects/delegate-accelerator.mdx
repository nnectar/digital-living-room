Designed and executed a structured delegate training curriculum for Scroll DAO, developing competent and diverse governance participants through hands-on assessment and protocol-specific education.

## Results

- **80** applicants for the program
- **30** delegates selected for the cohort
- **10-week** curriculum covering treasury management, proposal evaluation, and onchain governance
- Graduates appeared in [Scroll's Verified Delegates Cohort 1](https://forum.scroll.io/t/verified-delegates-cohort-1/1430)

## Governance Process

- [Original Proposal](https://forum.scroll.io/t/proposal-scroll-dao-delegate-accelerator-proposal/571) — authored with Kene on the Scroll governance forum
- [Execution Plan & Cohort Updates](https://forum.scroll.io/t/scroll-dao-delegate-accelerator-program-execution-plan/826) — program delivery August 4 – September 28, 2025
