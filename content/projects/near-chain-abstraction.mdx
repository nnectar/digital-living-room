Led developer education and go-to-market for NEAR's Chain Abstraction stack as part of NEAR's DevHub team. Designed hackathon tracks, educational workshops, and technical content that introduced developers to building cross-chain user experiences on NEAR — enabling applications where users interact across multiple blockchains without managing separate wallets or bridging assets manually.

## What Chain Abstraction Does

NEAR's chain abstraction layer lets developers build applications that work across blockchains transparently. Key primitives:

- **Chain Signatures** — MPC-based signing that lets NEAR accounts control addresses on any chain (Ethereum, Bitcoin, Cosmos, etc.)
- **Multichain Gas Relayer** — abstracts gas token management so users don't need native tokens on each chain
- **Account Aggregation** — unified identity layer connecting accounts across chains under a single NEAR account

## Key Programs

- Designed hackathon tracks and challenge briefs introducing chain abstraction concepts at events across San Francisco, New York, Lagos, Mexico City, Berlin, and Brussels
- Built educational workshop content translating chain abstraction architecture into practical builder-facing tutorials
- Ran developer onboarding sessions at NEAR Hacks events globally
