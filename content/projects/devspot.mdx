Shipped and scaled DevSpot as the developer onboarding and project submission platform powering global builder programs. Used DevSpot as the primary intake system for hackathons, bounties, and grant programs — managing the full lifecycle from builder registration through project submission to judging and prize distribution.

## Impact

- **8,700+** builders on the platform
- **2,200+** projects shipped
- **15** hackathons hosted
- **70+** countries represented across programs
- **$400K+** in prizes distributed through the platform

## What DevSpot Does

DevSpot connects builders, startups, investors, and organizations around hackathons, projects, and funding opportunities:

- **Builders** — ship projects, find hackathons, form teams
- **Organizations** — run hackathons, bounties, and grant programs
- **Investors** — evaluate founders through proof of work
- **Recruiters** — hire based on demonstrated skills
