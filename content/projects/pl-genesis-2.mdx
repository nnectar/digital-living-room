Led the GTM and marketing campaign for PL_Genesis Season 2: Frontiers of Collaboration, expanding the program's scope into frontier domains at the intersection of decentralized infrastructure and emerging science.

## Results

- **880** projects submitted
- **70+** countries represented
- **$150K+** in prizes distributed
- **4 tracks** spanning Web3, crypto, AI/robotics, and neurotech

## Program Structure

Four focus areas — Web3 & Digital Human Rights; Crypto & Decentralized Economies; AI, AGI & Robotics; and Neurotech & Human-Machine Interfaces — reflecting Protocol Labs' thesis that breakthroughs emerge from the collaboration layer between these systems.

## Sponsors

Filecoin, Flow, Impulse AI, Lit Protocol, NEAR, Physical AI, Starknet, Storacha, Zama, Ethereum Foundation, Hypercerts.

## Cumulative Impact (Seasons 1 & 2)

- **4,300+** builders participated
- **744+** projects submitted
- **$400K+** distributed in prizes
- **30+** ecosystem partners
