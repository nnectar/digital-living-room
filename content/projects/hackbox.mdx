Built Hackbox, a revolutionary plug-and-play hackathon platform empowering local leads and community stewards to host hackathons at scale. Productized the full developer activation stack: registration flows, judging tooling, project submission infrastructure, and Rust/AssemblyScript SDK onboarding for NEAR.

## Results

- **30+** hackathon deployments worldwide
- **1,500+** hackers across **40+** countries
- **$5K–$10K** allowance per hackathon plus bounty pools
- Cities: San Francisco, New York, Lagos, Mexico City, Guadalajara, Monterrey, Berlin, Brussels, Tulsa, Tunisia
