Built Hackbox as part of leading global growth marketing campaigns supporting 30+ developer conferences, hackathons, and ecosystem programs for NEAR Protocol. Designed acquisition strategies spanning partnerships, social campaigns, educational initiatives, ambassador programs, and ecosystem incentives — productizing the full activation funnel from awareness to registration to project submission. Built a scalable operational platform that improved developer onboarding, event execution, and long-term ecosystem retention, enabling repeatable developer acquisition across 10+ cities without centralized overhead.

Beyond running programs, Hackbox became the primary vehicle for bringing core components of NEAR's evolving tech stack to market. Designed hackathon tracks, challenge briefs, educational content, and workshop programming around **Chain Abstraction**, **NEAR AI**, and **NEAR Intents** — translating new protocol primitives into builder-facing narratives, practical use cases, and hands-on challenges that accelerated developer understanding and adoption of infrastructure that was still being defined.

## Results

- **30+** hackathon deployments worldwide
- **1,500+** hackers across **40+** countries
- **$5K–$10K** allowance per hackathon plus bounty pools
- Cities: San Francisco, New York, Lagos, Mexico City, Guadalajara, Monterrey, Berlin, Brussels, Tulsa, Tunisia

## Bringing NEAR's Tech Stack to Market

- **Chain Abstraction** — designed hackathon tracks and educational workshops that introduced developers to NEAR's chain abstraction layer, helping builders understand how to create cross-chain experiences without requiring users to manage multiple wallets or bridge assets manually
- **NEAR AI** — built developer education content and challenge programming around NEAR's AI stack, including autonomous agent frameworks and AI-native application patterns, culminating in events like the [One Trillion Agents Hackathon](https://1t-agents.devpost.com/) and the [AI CHAIN NEAR Hackathon](https://taikai.network/en/hackbox/hackathons/ai-chain-near-tunisia/overview) in Tunisia
- **NEAR Intents** — ran the [Zcash x NEAR Intents Hackathon](https://taikai.network/en/hackbox/hackathons/zcashxnear/overview) as a launch vehicle for NEAR's intents-based architecture, designing challenge tracks and technical resources that helped developers build on cross-chain intent resolution infrastructure
