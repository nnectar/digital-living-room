Led GTM for Founders Forge, coordinating acquisition campaigns, investor engagement, fundraising support, and demo day execution — turning hackathon participants into funded founders. Built the full program architecture from selection criteria to curriculum design to investor matchmaking, creating a repeatable flywheel from builder acquisition (hackathon) to product validation (accelerator) to capital deployment (Demo Day).

## Results

- **8 teams** in the cohort
- **12-week** program with modular, expert-led sessions
- **Demo Day in Dubai** during Build Week, connecting teams to **150+ investors**
- **Up to $30K** in non-dilutive grants per team, with potential follow-on funding
- **Viatika** won $52,500 from Flow/USDFC
- One team generated revenue from a live fishing tournament app ($31K prize from Protocol Labs)
- Crop insurance team won $26,500 from Protocol Labs and WeatherXM
