Led the first cohort of Founders Forge, Protocol Labs' 12-week accelerator that takes early-stage web3 and AI founders from concept to validated MVP to launch-ready startup. Serves as the post-hackathon pathway for top PL_Genesis teams.

## Results

- **8 teams** in the cohort
- **12-week** program with modular, expert-led sessions
- **Demo Day in Dubai** during Build Week, connecting teams to **150+ investors**
- **Up to $30K** in non-dilutive grants per team, with potential follow-on funding
- **Viatika** won $52,500 from Flow/USDFC
- One team generated revenue from a live fishing tournament app ($31K prize from Protocol Labs)
- Crop insurance team won $26,500 from Protocol Labs and WeatherXM

## Mentors

Juan Benet (founder, Protocol Labs / IPFS / Filecoin), Molly Mackinlay (CEO, FilOz), Sabeen Ali (founder, AngelHack).
