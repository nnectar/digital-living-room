Served as reviewer and analyst for the Uniswap Arbitrum Grants Program at StableLab, evaluating growth proposals and producing data-driven governance research that informed how the protocol allocated incentives across L2 deployments. Part of StableLab's broader institutional governance engagement with Uniswap, Arbitrum, and the cross-chain DeFi ecosystem.

## Work

- Reviewed and evaluated grant proposals for the Uniswap Revitalization and Growth Program
- Produced governance research analyzing STIP efficacy and LTIPP comparisons for the Arbitrum ecosystem
- Contributed to building the Uniswap Incentive Analysis Terminal — analytics covering incentive performance across Arbitrum, Base, Scroll, and Blast
- Authored and supported governance forum posts translating complex analytics into actionable recommendations
